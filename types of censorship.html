Types of Censorship

Here's a deeper breakdown of these major categories of censorship:

  1. Political Censorship
    Blocking speech that challenges government authority or exposes corruption.

  2. Moral Censorship
    Removing content considered immoral or inappropriate (violence, sexuality, profanity).

  3. Religious Censorship
    Suppressing material that opposes or contradicts religious beliefs.

  4. Corporate Censorship
    Companies removing or hiding content that harms their brand or profits.

  5. Educational Censorship
    Schools removing certain books, topics, or discussions.

  6. Artistic Censorship
    Editing or banning artwork or performances that may be controversial.


Each type has different motivations, and all of them can be debated.
