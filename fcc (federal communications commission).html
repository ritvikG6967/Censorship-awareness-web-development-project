The Federal Communications Commission (FCC)

The FCC regulates communication systems in the U.S., including television, radio, satellite, and some online services.

What the FCC does:

  • Regulates broadcast content
  • Enforces rules about profanity, nudity, or indecency
  • Issues fines for violations
  • Ensures emergency broadcasting works properly
  • Manages public airwaves

Examples of FCC censorship

  • Blur or beep profanity during daytime TV
  • Delay live awards shows to prevent accidental swear words
  • Restrict adult content to late-night hours
  • Fine radio hosts for inappropriate behavior

The FCC mainly affects broadcast media, not movie theaters or streaming services
