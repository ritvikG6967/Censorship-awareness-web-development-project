Social Media Censorship

Social platforms like Instagram, TikTok, X (Twitter), YouTube, and Facebook moderate and censor content.

Why they censor:

  • Stop misinformation
  • Reduce harmful or dangerous content
  • Enforce community guidelines
  • Protect minors
  • Avoid legal problems

Examples:

  • Removing hate speech and harassment
  • Taking down violent or graphic videos
  • Shadow banning harmful accounts
  • Flagging misleading posts

Social media censorship is controversial because companies, not governments, decide what stays online.
