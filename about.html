About this site

This website is designed for everyone to raise awareness for censorship no matter the age, gender, race, ethnicity, etc., and to educate what censorship is.

Everybody should have the right to freedom of speech, though not all countries give citizens that right. So it is important that we should all be aware how censorship can impact internet society wether it be in a helpful way or a negative way.

We are not here to promote suppresion on any educational and inspirational content that sometimes governments feel like it is deemed inappropriate without a valid reason, especially for children and teens.

If you have any suggestions on how to improve this site, please fill out the feedback form with the link located in the navigation menu.

This website was originally designed by college students from Towson University.
