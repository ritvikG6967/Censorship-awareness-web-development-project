MPAA (Movie Ratings)

The MPAA/MPA creates movie ratings in the U.S. to help parents understand film content.

Why do movie ratings exist?

  • To inform Parents
  • To provide age-appropriate guidance
  • To help theaters control who can see certain films
  • To warn viewers about potentially sensitive material

Rating Categories

G — General Audiences

No offensive content; suitable for all ages

PG — Parental Guidance Suggested

Some mild violence, language, or themes.

PG-13 — Parents Strongly Cautioned

More intense scenes, stronger language, and mature themes.

R — Restricted

Under 17 requires an adult. Includes strong language, violence, or sexual content.

NC-17 — Adults Only

No one under 17 allowed. Contains explicit adult material.

Criticism of MPAA:

  • Sometimes reports claim that they can be inconsistent
  • Stricter on sexual content than violence
  • Can affect film revenue and distribution
