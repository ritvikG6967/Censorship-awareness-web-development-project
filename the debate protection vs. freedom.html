The Debate: Protection vs. Freedom

This page explains both sides of the censorship argument.

Arguments FOR censorship

  • Protect children
  • Reduce exposure to violence or harmful ideas
  • Maintain social order
  • Protect national security
  • Prevent misinformation

Arguments AGAINST Censorship

  • Limits free speech
  • Hides important information
  • Prevents creativity and open discussion
  • Gives too much power to authorities
  • Can be abused for political control
